Questions

Is there a minimum order?

Most prints have no minimum, so you can order a single piece. Screen printing needs at least 24 pieces, and puff print and patches need at least 10.

How long does it take?

Standard delivery takes about 14 working days. Express (about 7) and rush (about 4) are available for an extra fee. Embroidery and patches can add a few days.

Do group members have to pay?

No. Members only add their size and name through your link. The organizer pays once for the whole group.

Will I see my design before it's printed?

Yes. We send a final proof for your approval, and nothing is printed until you approve it.

How can I pay?

Pay half up front by InstaPay or a mobile wallet, and the rest in cash when your order arrives.
